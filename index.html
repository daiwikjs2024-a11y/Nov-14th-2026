CHAPTER ONE · THE UNEXPECTED PLOT TWIST

01🌱

The shy era

I had just joined Brilliant Center and spent the first few months being the shy little kid who barely spoke to anyone. Then one day, I saw you talking to Kesav on the ground floor. I walked over, sat down, and gave you a “hi.”

And just like that, a friendship began. No dramatic music. No clue what was coming.

02🤡

The wrong turn

At first, we were just friends. Then I developed a crush on someone else, got rejected, and carried on with life like a perfectly normal main character.

In hindsight, thank you, universe, for the redirection. The plot was plotting.

03💌

November 14, 2025

One afternoon, before leaving the coaching center, you gave me a letter. It was so, so sweet. I took a little time to think about it… and then I said yes.

The best decision I’ve made. And my life has been better with you in it ever since.
